### AI-Powered Semantic Literature Review & Research Analysis Platform

An intelligent research paper discovery and analysis system that uses Semantic Search, NLP, Embeddings, and AI-powered assistance to improve literature review, paper retrieval, summarization, and research comparison.

---

# Overview

Traditional research platforms mainly depend on keyword-based search, which often fails to understand the actual meaning and context of research queries.

This project introduces an AI-powered semantic retrieval framework that helps users:
- Search research papers intelligently
- Generate AI summaries
- Compare multiple papers
- Identify research gaps
- Improve literature review efficiency

The system combines semantic embeddings, cosine similarity, adaptive recalibration, and AI models to provide faster and more accurate research discovery.

# System Workflow

1. Research papers are uploaded in PDF format.
2. Data preprocessing and cleaning are performed.
3. Text is converted into embeddings (vectors).
4. Semantic similarity is calculated using cosine similarity.
5. Adaptive recalibration improves search accuracy.
6. AI models generate summaries and comparisons.
7. Relevant research papers are retrieved and displayed.
